# Slirc blog

Notes from building Slirc, team chat with IRC underneath and room for your coding agents. Web version: https://slirc.org/blog/

- 21 November 2026, 3 min: [Slirc got eyes](https://slirc.org/blog/slirc-new-logo/). Slirc's logo was a terminal prompt in a speech bubble. Now it has the face of its helper bots, because Slirc is about talking, with friends and with agents.
- 18 November 2026, 4 min: [People are moving in](https://slirc.org/blog/people-are-moving-in/). Slirc grew almost four times in a week. Here's what new people found when they arrived, and what we shipped while they settled in.
- 16 November 2026, 6 min: [Software as a Coffee](https://slirc.org/blog/saac/). You can make coffee at home or you can go to a cafe. I think software can be sold the same way. It's open source if you want to host it, and a small fee if you don't want to do the dishes.
- 14 November 2026, 3 min: [Slirc agents, third try](https://slirc.org/blog/slirc-agents/). Getting coding agents into Slirc took longer than I expected, and I threw away two approaches on the way. Pi works today. Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode are next.
- 14 November 2026, 3 min: [Slirc for iPhone and Mac](https://slirc.org/blog/slirc-apps/). Slirc has a Mac app you can download, and the iPhone app is waiting on Apple's beta review. Both wrap the same web app, so there's one codebase.
- 22 October 2026, 2 min: [A Slirc company, slirc.app and a marathon](https://slirc.org/blog/quiet-weeks/). Why the blog went quiet after Slirc's pre-launch: Slirc has a company now, slirc.app is up, I ran my first marathon, in Amsterdam, and there's a big project at Miro. Agents are next.
- 5 October 2026, 2 min: [Slirc goes into pre-launch tomorrow](https://slirc.org/blog/prelaunch-tomorrow/). Slirc's invite-only pre-launch is tomorrow, four weeks after I started. I'm focusing on chat first. Agents will follow.
- 3 October 2026, 3 min: [My first patch to Ergo](https://slirc.org/blog/ergo/). A message sent to multiple IRC targets shared one ID. Ergo's delete lookup ignored the target channel, so I patched it.
- 1 October 2026, 6 min: [Slirc 2, new models (Astra, DSv4.1-flash)](https://slirc.org/blog/slirc2/). Expectation: 2 days. Reality: a full rewrite in 3 weeks. What I learned from Astra, DeepSeek v4.1 Flash and good old IRC.
- 29 September 2026, 9 min: [Slack + IRC = Slirc.org](https://slirc.org/blog/slirc/). A slim Slack built on top of IRC: threads as private channels, oEmbed and Giphy embeds, a PWA client for your home screen, and DMing your coding agent from your phone.
- 17 September 2026, 8 min: [I gave my coding agent an IRC account](https://slirc.org/blog/i-gave-my-coding-agent-an-irc-account/). Why Slirc exists, how threads, DMs, and uploads map onto IRC, what it costs to run next to Campfire, and how a coding agent became a member of the room.
