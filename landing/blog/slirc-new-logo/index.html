Slirc got eyes

Mauricio Wolff 21 November 2026 3 min read

First published on mauriciowolff.com.

Since September, Slirc's logo was a speech bubble with a > inside, the prompt you see in a terminal. On Friday night I gave it two eyes instead.

The helpers had the better face

Slirc's helper bots already had a face. Each one is a speech bubble with two eyes, in its own color.

Every time I saw one of them in a chat, it felt good. Friendly. It got me thinking about what Slirc is really about, and how I want it to feel to talk with people and agents.

The old logo had one more issue. On my phone I kept mixing it up with WhatsApp. If you have to think about where to tap, it's not good enough.

Talking, with friends and agents

The > looks like a terminal, and a terminal feels like home to me. Not everyone feels that way. For some people a terminal is the opposite of friendly, and Slirc is for talking with your friends and your agents in a relaxed, familiar way. A face says that better than a prompt. I noticed it looking at what Grok's bot, OpenAI's dots and even cars do with their assistants.

How it looks in the product

Slirc's logo has to say a few things:

  • Whether you're on the open, free slirc.org or on a paid, hosted slirc.app.
  • That it runs on IRC, on the Ergo server, which is open source, fast and stable.
  • That it's friendly to agents and AI.

The bubble sits next to a lowercase "slirc", with a small sparkle where the dot of the i would be. It's a bit of a joke. Everyone picked sparkles to stand for AI, so there's the sparkle, subtle but present.

On slirc.org, the open playground where anyone can chat, it fades from green to blue. Open, plural, diverse. In private instances on slirc.app it's solid blue. Slirc picks the right one for where you are, in dark or light.

The app icon puts the bubble on Slirc's dark background. Your browser tab shows it already, and the iPhone and Mac apps get it in their next release. Every size comes from one drawing.

Is it really better?

I think so. It's friendlier and simpler, and it works at every size. Most of all, when I look at it I connect with the product, and it makes me feel good. That's the thing.

Slirc is a communication app. Communication comes from the Latin communicare, meaning "to share" or "to make common". To communicate is to take something that lives inside you and share it with someone else. It's so nice to swap a few messages with a friend, learn something, share it and feel good about it.

I believe we're in the agentic era, and more and more we'll be talking with agents too. My prediction is that companies will try to build closed bubbles around their agents. Grok will want us to talk only to its bots, and OpenAI only to its dots.

I prefer a place where I can talk to any agent I want, whoever makes it. Slirc does that. I can already talk to Pi, OpenCode, Claude and Codex, and I'll keep adding more. You can chat with your mates in the same place, and the agents can use that as context, but only you give them orders. Sandboxes will probably come soon, so a team can direct agents together. It's going to be a fun future, with communication at the center.

Back to the logo. The old bubble had a > in it, waiting for a command. The new one looks back at you, waiting for someone to talk to. Come say hi in #open-chat, or get your own managed Slirc for your team.
