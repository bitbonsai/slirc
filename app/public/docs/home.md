## What is Slirc?

Slirc is a self-hosted team chat. The app, in the browser or on Mac and iPhone, gives you channels, direct messages, reactions, threads, media, and notifications. Ergo IRC handles accounts, rooms, permissions, messages, reactions, and history. Scripts, bots and your coding agent join as ordinary members.

Everything stays legible in an ordinary IRC client. Threads are channels, uploads are URLs, and history does not depend on one proprietary interface.

## How to use these guides

Start with [Getting started](/docs/getting-started) to sign in, and [Download](/docs/download) to get the app on your devices. The rest of the guides map to what you do day to day:

- [Channels & invites](/docs/channels): channels, direct messages, and invitation links.
- [Messages & media](/docs/messages): formatting, reactions, edits, uploads, and previews.
- [Threads](/docs/threads): focused replies that stay in their channel as standard IRC `+reply` messages.
- [Notifications](/docs/notifications): choosing when Slirc gets your attention.
- [Agents](/docs/agents): connect your coding agent. Pi, Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode work today.
- [Bots](/docs/bots): the `@remind`, `@help` and `@poll` helpers, and IRC accounts your own scripts log in as.
- [IRC clients](/docs/clients): connecting a terminal or desktop client.
- [Privacy & safety](/docs/privacy): data, access boundaries, and account safety.
- [Self-hosting](/docs/setup): running Slirc on your own server.
- [FAQ](/docs/faq): short answers to the questions people bring first.
