Guides / Getting started

Getting started

Sign up on a public instance or join from an invite email, and start chatting in a couple of minutes. Every step works on desktop and phone.

Join a public instance

On a public instance, open #open-chat and read along. When you want to post, tap the message box or choose Sign in to post. Type your email and, if you're new, what we should call you. Then open the link in the email, or type its code; pasting the code with its space works. You land in #open-chat.

Your nick comes from the name you typed, without spaces or symbols. Staff names like admin aren't available. A new account belongs to #open-chat and #help only, plus its DM with help, until someone invites it somewhere else. A signup nobody confirms within two hours is dropped.

Join from your invite

  1. Open the link in your invite email.
  2. Pick a display name and choose Join Slirc. If a friend invited you, the button says Join. There's no password to set.
  3. If you were invited to a channel, you land in it. Help sends you a DM with a link to your first steps. Reply there with any question about Slirc, any time.

Your display name also becomes your nick, the @handle next to your messages. You can change your display name any time under You; your nick stays the same. Staff and helper names, and the names of admins, agents and bots, aren't available.

Say hi in #open-chat

Open #open-chat under Channels, in the sidebar on desktop or on Home on a phone. Type your message at the bottom and press Enter or the send button.

On a phone, pull down at the top of Home, More or a conversation to check you're up to date. Slirc confirms the connection, reconnects if it dropped, and fetches anything new. With no network it says you're offline.

Make a channel

  1. Choose + next to Channels, in the desktop sidebar or on phone Home.
  2. Give it a name and pick Invite-only or Open. Only people you invite can see an invite-only channel. Open channels are listed for every member to join.
  3. Choose Create channel. You land in the new channel, ready to invite people.

Bring a friend

In a channel, type /invite friend@example.com and choose Invite by email in the note that appears. Your friend gets an email with a link to join that channel.

To start a DM, choose + next to Direct messages, in the desktop sidebar or on the phone DMs screen. Type a name to find someone who's already here, or an email address to invite someone new and choose Send invite. The dialog shows how many email invites you have left; admins have no limit.

Send messages, photos, GIFs and videos

  • Messages: press Enter to send and Shift + Enter for a new line. Markdown basics like **bold** and *italic* work too.
  • Photos: choose + beside the message box and pick up to 10 JPEG, PNG or WebP photos, each under 12 MB. You can also paste photos into the message box, and on desktop drop them onto the conversation or the message box.
  • GIFs: type /giphy and a word, like /giphy hello, then pick a GIF.
  • Videos: choose + beside the message box and pick an MP4, MOV or WebM video. A large video is compressed to about 720p before upload and has to end up under 30 MB. It plays right in the conversation. Longer videos are better shared as a YouTube, Vimeo or Loom link, which also plays inline.

Start threads and react

To keep a side conversation out of the channel, type /threads followed by your reply. That starts a thread from the latest message. On desktop you can also hover any message, choose ⋮, then Reply in thread. Your threads appear under Threads in the sidebar or on Home.

To react on desktop, hover a message and choose the smile button. On any device, tap a reaction under a message to add yours, and tap it again to take it back.

Change your profile picture

Choose your avatar, at the bottom of the sidebar on desktop or at the top right on a phone. Choose the picture at the top of your profile and pick an image. Your new profile picture saves right away.

Turn on notifications

Open your profile by choosing your avatar on desktop, or More → Profile on a phone. Choose Change beside Notifications and allow them when your browser or the app asks. On iPhone in Safari, add Slirc to your Home Screen first (see below), since Safari sends notifications only to the Home Screen app.

Good to know

Only members post in #open-chat and #help. On a public instance, a visitor who isn't signed in can read recent #open-chat messages at /open-chat: the text, who wrote it and when, and nothing else. Only signed-in members can read #help, and it keeps participant presence hidden in the Slirc app.

Questions, bugs or ideas? Ask @help or message your instance's admin inside Slirc. For more detail, read channels and invites and messages and media.

Save Slirc to your Home Screen

Once it's on your Home Screen, Slirc opens from its icon, like any other app.

iPhone

Open Slirc in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

If your instance offers the iPhone app in TestFlight, signed-in members find the link under More → Get the iPhone app on a phone. See Download for details.

Android

Open the browser menu and choose Add to Home screen or Install app.

Add your phone

On a computer where you're signed in, choose Add your phone in the sidebar, or open Devices from your profile and choose Show code. A QR code appears. Point your phone's camera at it and open the link. The phone signs in to the same account, with nothing to type.

The code works once and lasts a minute. A fresh one replaces it while the card stays open. When the phone gets in, the card names its browser and system. If that wasn't you, choose Not you? Sign it out.

If you saved Slirc to your iPhone Home Screen, open that app and choose Scan to sign in on its sign-in screen. The Home Screen app keeps its own sign-in, separate from Safari, so it has its own scanner.

Sign in on another device

On the new device, open Slirc and choose Link with another device, or Use a code instead on a phone. Type the code it shows under Devices on a device where you're already signed in, then choose Review and Approve.

No other device handy? Enter your email, choose Send sign-in link, and open the link from that email on the new device.
